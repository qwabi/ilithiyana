Client website meeting facilitation guide with question sections

5 phases ~30 questions website → web app scoping
1
Current website — what exists today Foundation
  • Can you walk us through your current website — what it does and who uses it?
  • What platform or technology is it built on? (WordPress, Wix, custom code, etc.)
  • Who manages and maintains it right now?
  • What's working well that you want to keep?
  • What's frustrating you or your users about it currently?
Ask them to share their screen if remote — seeing the live site avoids misunderstandings.
2
Desired changes — what they want Core scope
  • What specific changes are you asking for? Walk me through them one by one.
  • For each change — is this a design/visual change, a content change, or a functional/behaviour change?
  • Do you have examples, screenshots, or competitor sites that show what you're envisioning?
  • Are these must-haves or nice-to-haves? Can you rank them?
  • Who will the end users of these changes be — same audience or a new one?
Document each change in a shared notes doc as they speak. Confirm your understanding after each one before moving on.
3
Web app scope — the big shift Key phase
  • What makes this more than a website — what should users be able to do that they can't do now?
  • Will users need to create accounts or log in?
  • Will the app store, retrieve, or process user data?
  • Are there different roles or permission levels? (e.g. admin vs regular user)
  • Does it need to connect to any external systems? (payments, CRMs, APIs, databases)
  • Will users interact with each other, or is it each user working independently?
  • Does anything need to happen in real time? (notifications, live updates, chat)
This is where website becomes web app. Each "yes" here adds significant scope — flag it clearly so the client understands the implications on cost and timeline.
4
Technical & business constraints Boundaries
  • Do you have a preferred technology stack, or are you open to recommendations?
  • Are there any existing systems this must integrate with?
  • What's the rough budget range you're working with?
  • Who will own and maintain this after it's built — your team or ours?
  • Are there any compliance or data privacy requirements? (POPIA, GDPR, etc.)
  • Does this need to work on mobile, or is desktop sufficient?
Budget and ownership questions are often uncomfortable but essential — get them on the table early to avoid scope creep or handover surprises.
5
Timeline, delivery & success Wrap-up
  • Is there a hard deadline, or a preferred go-live date?
  • Are there any upcoming events or launches this needs to be ready for?
  • How would you like to stay updated during the project? (weekly calls, Slack, email)
  • Who on your side has final sign-off on designs and builds?
  • How will we know this project has been successful — what does a win look like for you?
End the meeting by summarising what you've heard and confirming next steps — who sends the brief, who does the follow-up, and by when.
