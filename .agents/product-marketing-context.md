## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Small groups. Real results. Fully online.

**What it does:** Ilithiyana Academics provides structured online group tutoring for Grade 6–12 South African learners on the CAPS curriculum. Sessions run on Google Meet with no travel or in-person component. Families join a managed programme: tutor assigned, schedule set, career guidance included, admin handled. Applications and payments run through the website (PayFast).

**Product category:** Online tutoring / supplementary education (CAPS-aligned).

**Product type:** Service business with web application for applications, subscriptions, portals, and admin.

**Business model:**
- **Package A (recommended):** R1,000/month — 8 lessons/month across up to 4 subjects + weekly career guidance; monthly recurring (PayFast subscription)
- **Package B:** R175/lesson — pay per session + career guidance access; once-off or bundle; exam prep / try-before-commit
- **Format:** 1 tutor : max 3 learners; 1 hour per session; day/time agreed with parent around school schedule; recurring weekly schedule
- Pricing is flat across subjects and grades today; future differentiation planned (EFAL, FET, Grade 12 prep)

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** South African parents see their child falling behind in Maths or Science while school classes and informal tutors fail to deliver consistent, personal attention.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **School extra classes:** Overcrowded, inconsistent
- **Private 1:1 tutors (R150–R350/hr):** Expensive, cancellations, no career support, no programme structure
- **Marketplaces (Superprof, TutorHunt, etc.):** Browse, compare, self-manage — no quality control or consistency
- **Franchises (Kip McGrath):** English/Maths only at many centres; no Sciences; quality varies; no career guidance
- **Premium 1:1 (Tutor Doctor, Genius):** High cost; no group motivation; no bundled career component

**What it costs them:** Time coordinating tutors; money on ineffective help; emotional stress around matric and university entry; geographic isolation from good tutors.

**Emotional tension:** Panic before matric; guilt at not being able to help with changed curriculum; fear of repeating a grade; frustration with hit-and-miss tutoring.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators (prioritised):**
1. **1:3 ratio** — intentional model; child seen and heard every session (not a classroom, not cost-cutting)
2. **Career guidance included** — weekly Monday sessions (university apps, subject choices, bursaries, life-after-school); not an add-on; rare at this price
3. **Fully online, any province** — same quality in Limpopo as Johannesburg; no travel
4. **Managed programme** — not a marketplace; tutor assigned, schedule set, admin handled
5. **Affordable structured programme** — R1,000/month for 8 sessions + career guidance; the same R1,000 buys only 4 private hours at R250/hr with no career support
6. **Registered business** — Reg. 2020/652431/07; POPIA compliant; 30-minute onboarding for every family

Unlike marketplaces where you search and hope, Ilithiyana is a managed programme: tutor assigned, schedule set, career guidance included, admin handled.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “R1,000 is a lot.” | 8 hours tutoring + weekly career guidance. Private tutor at R250/hr ≈ R2,000 for same hours with no career support. |
| “I want 1-on-1, not a group.” | Max 3 learners — not a class; your child is heard every session; small-group peer learning by design. |
| “How do I know it will help?” | Tutors produce term reports: where the child improved, still struggles, and focus for next term. |
| “What about holidays / breaks?” | Package A is monthly; pause when not needed; no full-year lock-in. |
| “We're in a rural province.” | 100% online; device + internet; learners from multiple provinces already. |
| “I've never done online classes.” | 30-minute onboarding: how classes work, how to access links, what to expect. |

**Anti-persona:**
- Parents needing **Accounting, Geography, History, CAT, Afrikaans, Business Studies** (not offered — be upfront)
- Parents expecting **1:1 private tutoring at R1,000/month** (model is 1:3 — communicate upfront to avoid churn)
- **IEB/Cambridge** families (different needs — defer until product ready)
- Legacy **fleet / infrastructure** buyers (out of scope)
- Adult / university learners outside Gr 6–12
